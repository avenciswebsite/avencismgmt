## 3. Analysis of Visual Relationships & Screen Pairings

During the audit, three pairs of closely related screens were analyzed to understand their functional distinctions:

1. **`01-employees-directory` (ID `4f1d5879d9ac4234afcfa38886e250e0`) vs `15-employee-directory` (ID `545bff9e19194977901c102bdcd1b348`):**
   * Screen `01-employees-directory` is an **Admin Master Directory** featuring comprehensive multi-tenant filters (Legal Entity dropdown, Client account selector, Branch location filter, Employment status toggles, and Quick Add action).
   * Screen `15-employee-directory` represents a **Client Manager / Team Directory View** displaying filtered deployed headcount without salary attributes, suitable for scoped client-side supervisors.
2. **`06-payroll-processing` (ID `b48bf0b9951b4c9e8b8a546fbab4b8a8`) vs `06-payroll-guided-processing` (ID `e90ca6a75bc34ffeb591065e106a9911`):**
   * Screen `06-payroll-processing` (Batch 01) represents the **Executive Payroll Register Overview** showing aggregate monthly disbursements, bank account reconciliation status, and statutory totals.
   * Screen `06-payroll-guided-processing` (Batch 02) represents the **Interactive Step-by-Step Payroll Wizard** (Step 1: Attendance Finalisation -> Step 2: Leave & LWP Deduction -> Step 3: Incentives -> Step 4: Statutory Deductions -> Step 5: Approval & Lock).
3. **`13-employee-personal-information` (ID `b808814eaec44fdfa2d3399ba36debac`) vs `14-employee-profile-overview` (ID `e16e6a95e8cd470c81eece3434043f36`):**
   * Screen `13-employee-personal-information` provides the **Deep KYC & Profile Form** (Personal, Emergency contacts, Bank details, Identity proofs, and Compliance documents).
   * Screen `14-employee-profile-overview` provides the **Operational Summary Card** (Current shift, Assigned Client, Reporting Manager, Leave balance snapshot, and Recent attendance mini-timeline).
